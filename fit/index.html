Exercise Battle

Every rep grows your dragon.

OneTraxxFit turns a workout into a live battle. Hold your phone, copy your trainer on screen, and every rep you do grows your dragon by one section. In the break the dragons fight. Five rounds, one winner. Play solo against up to five others, 3 v 3 with friends, or beat your own past self.

Features

A workout that fights back.

No wearable, no camera. Your phone in your hand is the controller.

01

Reps counted by motion

The phone's accelerometer and gyroscope count every full rep. Half moves and shakes do not count, and the coach tells you why.

02

One rep, one section

Every rep grows your dragon by one section. Six players, six nests, six dragons growing at once.

03

Fights after every round

In the break the dragons leave their nests. The longest wins the round and keeps its margin; the rest burn down and start again.

04

Weather that turns

Calm nests, then wind, then a thunderstorm for the last fight. The arena escalates with the match.

05

Solo, team or friends

Quick solo arena for up to six, team 3 v 3, or a private arena by code. Dragons fill empty nests so you never wait.

06

Beat Yourself

Race your own past match and set a new personal best when nobody else is around.

07

Bella, your coach

A voice coach counts you in, names the next exercise and calls out a move that was too small to count.

08

Stats that stick

Reps, sections eaten and calories per match, daily totals, personal bests, history and a profile card with your dragon.

09

Win and play free

Every match costs one credit. The winner gets it back, and three wins in a row earn a bonus match that never expires.

How It Works

From nest to winner in five rounds.

1

Pick an arena

Solo, Team, Play with friends or Beat Yourself. Guests get five free matches before an account is needed.

2

Hold the phone, copy the trainer

A 30-second round, one exercise drawn from six. Your trainer loops on screen; match the movement and the reps count.

3

Watch the fight

In the break the dragons fight tail for tail. The longest wins the round and carries its margin into the next.

4

Five rounds, most points wins

Round wins, kills and work at the cap all score. The storm rolls in for the fifth fight and the match goes to the highest total.

5

Collect and go again

The winner's credit comes back, streaks earn bonus matches, and your stats and history update on the spot.

The six

Exercises in the draw.

Five of the six are drawn for every match, one per round. All are done standing, phone in hand, in a space the size of a yoga mat.

Overhead knee chopPhone high, chop down to a raised knee.
Squat jumpDrop, explode up, land soft.
Jumping jackArms and legs out and in, the classic.
SkippingHop on the spot like a rope is turning.
Skater jumpSide to side hops, landing on one foot.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what OneTraxxFit collects, why, and the control you have over it. It applies to the OneTraxxFit app on Android and iOS and to this page.

1
Who We Are

OneTraxxFit is an exercise battle game operated by Vijay Dipchand Ahire, sole proprietor trading as OneTraxx, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra 410206, India. OneTraxxFit is a sub-brand of ONETRAXX. Questions about this policy go to info@onetraxx.com.

2
What We Collect

We collect only what the game needs to run and improve.

  • Account data — your email address and a password when you create an account. If you sign in with Google or Apple we receive the name and email those services share with us. If you play as a guest we create an anonymous account with a random Guest name and no email; you can attach an email later.
  • Profile data — username, dragon colour, an optional profile picture, an optional short bio, an optional region, and optional body weight and height used only to estimate calories. You choose what to fill in and whether your profile is visible to other players.
  • Fitness and match data — reps counted per round, calorie estimates, match results, points, personal bests, daily totals, streaks, level and trophies. This powers your stats, history and the leaderboards inside a match.
  • Motion sensor data — during a round the app reads your phone's accelerometer and gyroscope to count reps. Counting happens on the phone; each counted rep is sent to our match server so the other players see your dragon grow. On Android, counting continues while the screen is locked during a match; a notification shows while it runs and it stops when the match ends. Short motion traces (acceleration samples from a round) may also be sent to our server to improve rep detection. They are linked to your account id and contain no other personal detail.
  • Chat messages — text you send in a lobby or during a break is relayed live to the players in that arena. It is not stored after the match.
  • Friends and social data — friend requests, your friends list, arena invites and your online status.
  • Notifications — a push token for your device if you turn notifications on, used for invites and friend requests.
  • Purchase status — whether a Pro subscription is active. Payment itself is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details.
  • Device and crash data — app version, update id, device OS and error reports, collected to fix bugs. Crash reports go to a server we run ourselves.
3
What We Do Not Collect
  • We do not collect your precise location. The optional region on your profile is text you type yourself.
  • We do not record audio. Voice chat is not available in the current version; the microphone is never used unless a future version adds a voice button you choose to tap.
  • We do not sell your data, show advertising or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not read your contacts, calendar, photos (other than the one picture you pick for your profile) or any data outside the permissions you grant.
  • We do not collect medical records or connect to Health Connect or Apple Health. Calorie figures are estimates from your rep count and the body weight you optionally enter, and are not medical advice.
4
How We Use Your Data
  • To run the game — sign you in, seat you in an arena, count and relay your reps, decide the winner, save your history and credits.
  • To show you to other players — your username, dragon colour and picture appear in the lobbies and arenas you join. With "Profile visible" off, only your username shows.
  • To improve rep counting — motion traces are used to tune the exercise detector so honest reps count and shakes do not.
  • To keep the game fair — rep timing and motion checks flag impossible rep rates.
  • To fix bugs — crash and error reports.
  • To notify you — invites and friend requests, only if you turn notifications on.
5
Who We Share It With

We share data only with the services that run the app, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Expo (EAS) — delivers app updates and push notifications.
  • Google and Apple — sign-in, if you choose it, and in-app purchases through their stores.
  • RevenueCat — validates Pro subscriptions once Pro is on sale.
  • Our own servers — the live match relay and crash reporting run on infrastructure we operate.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

6
Storage, Security and Retention
  • Data travels encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets) and is stored with per-user access rules so one player cannot read another's private fields.
  • Your profile, stats and history are kept while your account exists.
  • Chat messages are not stored after a match. Live match state is discarded when the match ends.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, match history, records, friends and Pro status flag are removed immediately. Backups roll off within 30 days.
7
Your Choices and Rights
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings › Delete account. Or email us, see Delete account below.
  • Privacy settings in the app: hide your profile, choose who can send friend requests, hide your region.
  • Notifications can be turned off in the app or your phone's settings.
  • Access, correction or a copy of your data: email info@onetraxx.com from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days.
8
Children

OneTraxxFit is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

We may update this policy as the game grows. The effective date below changes when we do, and the app points to this page.
